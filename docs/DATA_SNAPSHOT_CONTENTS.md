the checked snapshot, not a general guarantee that arbitrary local files are safe.

verifies every listed file and will not overwrite a different existing local file.

historical artifacts intentionally have their own date ranges.

Source credentials remain private. Frozen experimental source/evidence hashes
must continue to be respected. Withdrawn V96 artifacts, quarantined models and
failed experiments remain historical records in output/data, not adopted results.

Files being downloadable does not certify financial accounting, forecast skill,
PPO performance or a successful full current-strategy reproduction.
