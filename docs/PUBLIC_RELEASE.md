## Current Adopted Accounting Research

V95 excludes 123015.SZ, 128100.SZ, 110072.SH, 128085.SZ, 128097.SZ and 127015.SZ
before basket construction. It preserves 38 labels and 12 convertible-bond baskets.
The 2018-01-02 to 2026-10-14 execution replay completed, but 4,660 missing
holding-valuation records and registered unconfirmed receivables remain.
End-of-period unresolved positions include 113024.SH and 113575.SH.

These are accounting diagnostics, not certified portfolio returns. A missing
valuation can reflect suspension or terminal-event timing rather than corrupt
market data; it is not sufficient reason to remove a whole historical security.
The blanket V96 screen was withdrawn for that reason.

No new model training, formal PPO promotion or three-product performance
certification is asserted by this snapshot. Known ordinary-coupon incompleteness
is not solved merely by excluding six securities.
